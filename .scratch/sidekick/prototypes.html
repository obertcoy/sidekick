Ticket strike (prototype, not in the repo)

From scratchpad/mod/hooks/register.tsx: when a ticket finishes, the critter zaps and a bolt ↯ runs down to the row, which flashes, has its strike-through burn left to right, then settles as done. One step every 110 ms, 13 steps (1.43 s), in step with the critter’s 12-frame zap. Each run starts with 5 ticks of the steady pane so the “before” state reads.
